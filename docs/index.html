Common questions

Where is my progress saved?

On your device, together with your coin balance, helpers, decorations, and settings. Boxmello has no game account or its own cloud sync. Device backups may contain app data depending on your Apple settings, but they are not a guaranteed Boxmello recovery service.

Please contact support before deleting or reinstalling the app to fix a problem. Doing so can lose your progress and unused coins.

How do I restore Mint Post?

Use the Apple Account that originally bought it, connect to the internet, and choose Restore Purchases in the in-game Shop or Settings. This checks eligible permanent cosmetic purchases, including Mint Post. It does not rebuild your level progress or consumable coin balance.

A purchase is pending or has not appeared. What should I do?

Do not pay again for the same missing purchase. Check your connection and whether Apple is waiting for a payment or family approval. Reopen Boxmello so it can retry verified deliveries. For Mint Post, also try Restore Purchases. If it remains unresolved, email the developer with the product name, approximate purchase time, app version, and the message shown. Redact unrelated receipt information.

Can I restore coin packs or move my progress to another device?

Coin packs are consumable purchases. Restore Purchases does not recreate previously delivered coins, spent items, or local progress. There is no account-based wallet or device-to-device save synchronization. Changing devices or deleting the app may lose your remaining coins and progress.

How do refunds work?

Apple handles payment and refund requests for App Store purchases. Visit Apple’s Report a Problem service or read Apple’s refund instructions. Eligibility depends on the purchase and applicable law. A verified refund may remove the associated cosmetic entitlement or relevant unspent coins.

Does Boxmello need an internet connection?

Normal puzzle play uses content bundled with the app and can run offline. Loading App Store products, making purchases, or restoring permanent purchases needs access to Apple’s services. Support email and this website also need a connection.

The game freezes, crashes, or does not save. What should I try?

Close and reopen Boxmello, check available device storage, and install any available app and iOS updates. If a save error appears, keep the app installed and send support the exact message. Avoid repeatedly purchasing or deleting the app while we investigate.

How can I change language or accessibility options?

Open in-game Settings for language, music, sound, haptics, reduced motion, and enhanced outlines. If text, controls, or VoiceOver are difficult to use, send your device model and a description of the screen to support.

How do I delete my data?

There is no Boxmello account to delete. Local app data, device backups, support emails, and Apple’s purchase records are handled separately. Follow the data and deletion guide for each.
