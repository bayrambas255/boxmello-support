YOUR CONTROL

Your data & deletion

There is no Boxmello account. Your device save, support messages, and third-party records each have a different deletion path.

Effective and last updated: 14 October 2026 · v1.0

1. Before deleting

Deleting the app can permanently lose progress and unused coins. If you are trying to fix a problem, contact support first. Restore Purchases restores eligible permanent cosmetics, not your coin balance or puzzle progress.

There is no dedicated in-game erase or account-deletion button. The developer has no server copy of your save and cannot remotely reset or recover it.

2. Remove local app data

  1. Open iPhone or iPad Settings → General → iPhone Storage / iPad Storage → Boxmello.
  2. Choose Delete App and confirm. Offload App keeps documents and data, so it is not the same action.
  3. Check other devices where Boxmello is installed; each device can have its own local save.

This removes Boxmello’s app container from that device, including its local saves and recovery files. Reinstalling creates a new save unless an operating-system backup or migration restores earlier data. This does not erase your App Store purchase history.

3. Manage backups separately

App data may remain in iCloud or computer backups according to your settings. Review Boxmello under your device’s iCloud backup settings, and remove or exclude its backup data if you want that copy deleted too. Review any Mac or PC backups separately. Apple’s menu labels can vary with operating-system version; see Apple’s iCloud storage guide. Deleting a backup can affect recovery of other data, so review what you select.

4. Ask to delete support correspondence

Email bayrambas255@icloud.com with the subject Boxmello privacy request. Use the email address previously used to contact support, if possible, and state:

  • Your name and surname.
  • The request: deletion, access, correction, or another privacy right.
  • The correspondence or approximate dates it concerns.
  • A reply address if different from the sending address.

Do not send a password, full payment card number, or an identity document unsolicited. Reasonable verification or details required by the applicable legal procedure may be requested. We will delete or anonymize developer-held data when the legal conditions are met, and explain any data that must be retained for a legal obligation or claim. Requests are answered within the legally applicable deadline, including at most 30 days under KVKK.

Simple support and deletion requests can use the method above. A formal KVKK application should be in Turkish and sent from an email address previously supplied to and registered with the developer, or use another method allowed by the applicable communiqué. Article 5 requires formal applications to include the prescribed details, such as name, request, service address, and relevant identity information. See the official application procedure. Do not send an identity document copy unsolicited.

5. Apple and GitHub records

Deleting Boxmello or emailing its developer does not erase Apple’s billing history, Apple Account data, or GitHub’s hosting security logs. For records they control, use Apple’s Data and Privacy tools or the contact methods in GitHub’s Privacy Statement. Any Apple backup copy must also be managed through your Apple settings.

Deletion of a local save does not itself cancel a purchase, request a refund, or revoke a valid permanent cosmetic entitlement. Apple may make that entitlement available again through Restore Purchases.

6. More about your rights

The Privacy Policy explains processing purposes, recipients, legal grounds, retention, and your KVKK and other applicable privacy rights. You may contact the relevant data protection authority under the applicable complaint procedure.
