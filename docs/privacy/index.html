YOUR PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

A practical explanation of Boxmello’s local saves, Apple services, support email, and this website. This is also our notice under Türkiye’s Personal Data Protection Law (KVKK).

Effective and last updated: 14 October 2026 · v1.0

1. Who is responsible?

Boxmello is provided by Bayram Baş in Türkiye. For personal data handled by the developer, Bayram Baş is the data controller. Contact: bayrambas255@icloud.com.

This policy covers the current iOS/iPadOS game, this support website, and correspondence with the developer. Apple and GitHub also process information for their own services under their own privacy notices.

2. Data stored in the game

Normal App Store gameplay does not send your game save to a developer server. Boxmello has no game account, advertising SDK, third-party analytics SDK, or cross-app tracking. It does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, or microphone.

The app stores a randomly generated local profile identifier, level progress, scores and stars, active puzzle state, coin and helper balances, decorations, daily rewards, tutorial status, and settings. It also stores purchase verification records such as product and transaction identifiers, processing dates, refund status, and permanent purchase entitlements. These let the app save your game, avoid duplicate purchase delivery, and apply verified purchase changes.

The save and its recovery copy are on the same device. They are not an independent cloud backup. Apple’s device backup or migration features may include this data depending on your settings. The developer has no remote access to your local save.

Local save and StoreKit information are processed electronically and automatically on the device to provide the game and verify purchases, on the contractual-performance ground in KVKK Article 5(2)(c).

3. Purchases and Apple-provided information

Apple handles App Store payments through StoreKit. Boxmello receives verified transaction and entitlement information on your device to deliver purchases and handle restoration or refunds. The developer does not receive your payment card details or Apple Account password.

Apple may make sales summaries and app performance or crash reports available to the developer. Usage and crash sharing depends on Apple’s services and your device choices; this is separate from an analytics SDK in Boxmello. See App Analytics & Privacy and Apple’s Privacy Policy. You can review sharing under iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements.

Developer-held diagnostic reports are used to identify and fix faults, based on legitimate interests under KVKK Article 5(2)(f), balanced against your rights. Copies held by the developer are kept only while needed to investigate and resolve the issue or meet a legal obligation or claim, then deleted or anonymized. Apple controls retention of reports in its own services.

4. Support correspondence and legal grounds

If you email the developer, your email address, sender name, message, and any attachments or technical details you choose to provide are received through iCloud Mail. Providing an email address and enough information about your question is needed for an email reply; you can play without contacting us. Please avoid sending sensitive personal information.

Support data is used to answer your request, investigate an issue, help with purchases, improve the reported problem, or handle a privacy request. It is not used for advertising, mailing lists, sale of personal data, or automated decisions about you.

5. Website hosting and cookies

This static support site has no developer-added cookies, local storage, advertising, contact forms, embedded social widgets, or visitor analytics. Images and styling are served with the site; no third-party font service is used.

GitHub Pages hosts the site. GitHub states that it logs visitors’ IP addresses for security, including visits made while signed out. GitHub controls those hosting logs; the developer does not receive an individual visitor log through this site. See GitHub Pages data collection and GitHub’s Privacy Statement. Following an external link takes you to that provider’s service.

6. Recipients and processing outside your country

Apple provides payment, platform reporting, device backup where enabled, and iCloud Mail services. GitHub provides website hosting and security. Support correspondence is handled by Bayram Baş and the email services used to deliver it. Information may also be disclosed to competent authorities when legally required, or to professional advisers where necessary for a legal claim, limited to what is needed.

Support messages are received and stored through Apple’s iCloud Mail; message content, sender information, and attachments may be processed on infrastructure outside Türkiye or your country of residence. GitHub also processes visitor IP logs on its international infrastructure to serve and secure this site. Apple’s and GitHub’s privacy notices explain those services. Visiting this site or emailing support is not treated as consent to an international transfer. Questions about a specific transfer involving developer-held data can be sent to the contact above.

7. Retention and security

Local saves remain on your device until the app data is removed; device or cloud backup copies are controlled separately. Support correspondence is retained only as needed to resolve the request and related follow-up, meet an applicable legal obligation, or establish, exercise, or defend a legal claim. The relevant period depends on the issue, any unresolved dispute, and the legally applicable retention or limitation period. Data no longer needed for those purposes is deleted or anonymized.

Apple and GitHub determine retention for records they control. Reasonable safeguards are used for developer-held correspondence; no storage or transmission method can guarantee complete security. Do not include passwords, full card details, or unnecessary identity documents in a support message.

8. Your privacy rights

Under KVKK Article 11, you may ask whether your data is processed; request information about its processing, purposes and use; learn domestic or overseas recipients; request correction, deletion or destruction where the legal conditions are met; and ask that recipients be notified of those corrections or deletions. You may object to an adverse result based exclusively on automated analysis and seek compensation for unlawful processing. Boxmello does not make such automated decisions.

Other applicable laws may also give access, portability, restriction, objection, and consent-withdrawal rights. You may complain to the relevant data protection authority. For Türkiye, see the KVKK rights guidance.

Email the developer from the address used in your correspondence, explaining your request. We may request proportionate verification and any details required by the applicable procedure. Requests are answered within the applicable legal time limit, including no later than 30 days under KVKK. A legally required retention exception will be explained. See the data and deletion guide. The developer cannot inspect or remotely erase a save held only on your device, or erase records controlled by Apple or GitHub.

9. Children and families

The game does not require children or other players to provide an account or personal details to play. We do not ask children to send personal information or use their data for advertising. A parent or guardian should handle support and purchase questions for a child. If you believe a child has sent personal information, contact the developer so it can be reviewed and deleted where appropriate. Follow the age rating displayed on the App Store and applicable parental permission requirements.

10. Optional beta and playtest sessions

TestFlight or development builds may contain an optional researcher-enabled playtest mode. When enabled, it writes a participant code, gameplay events, timing, and build information to local files. These are not automatically uploaded; an experimenter can deliberately export them through the device’s share sheet. The normal App Store version does not enable this mode. Any organized study must explain its purpose, recipients, retention, and participation choices before collecting or exporting study data. Apple’s TestFlight service has its own privacy terms.

11. Changes and contact

The date above identifies this version. This notice will be updated when data practices change; any additional notice or consent required by law must be provided before the relevant new processing. Questions and privacy requests: bayrambas255@icloud.com.
